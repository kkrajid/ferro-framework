# Code of conduct

Ferro wants to be a project where anyone can ask a question, report a problem or propose a
change and be treated with respect, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Discuss code and ideas, not people. Assume good intent; ask before you assume.
- Give feedback that helps: say what is wrong and, when you can, how to make it better.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a change, and explain why when they do.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a discussion after being asked to stop.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the
project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately through GitHub (@kkrajid), or use the repository's private
security reporting if the matter is sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially. Depending on
what happened, the maintainers may ask someone to change their behaviour, remove content, or
block them from the project.
